Living backgrounds

Your coastline, live, in every kind of weather.

Lunatide flies in over a 3D satellite view of your actual coast, then slowly circles it. The sky follows the real sun, going deep blue at night and gold at sunset, and the live National Weather Service conditions play out on top: drifting fog banks, passing clouds, rain, lightning and snow. The water at the bottom rises and falls with the tide itself.

  • Real weather: fog, clouds, rain, storms, haze and snow
  • Day, golden hour, twilight and night lighting from the sun's true position
  • Waves that sit higher or lower with the live tide level
  • Gentle and battery-friendly, and still when Reduce Motion is on
Tides

Know the water before you get there.

The current height, whether it's rising or falling, and a live countdown to the next high or low, from official NOAA predictions for the station nearest you.

  • 24-hour tide chart with day and night shading
  • Plus and minus heights, change per tide, and minus-tide badges
  • Tide flow: incoming or outgoing, fastest flow and slack water
  • Two weeks saved on your phone, so it works with no signal
Surf

Know which break is firing before you paddle out.

Lunatide forecasts the named surf breaks near you, from Rincon to Ditch Plains to the Abacos, and ranks them so the best spot right now is always on top. Each break is rated from Flat to Epic using the direction it actually faces, so offshore and onshore wind are judged the way surfers judge them.

  • Up to six nearby breaks, ranked live, with a crown on today's best
  • Tap any break to see its coastline, sessions and 7-day forecast
  • Wave height, swell period and direction, and wind, hour by hour
  • "Surf's up" alerts that name the break, about 90 minutes before it's good
Boating

Check the water before you leave the dock.

The official National Weather Service marine forecast for your stretch of coast, turned into something you can read at a glance: a clear Calm, Moderate, Choppy or Rough rating, wind and seas for every period, and any active advisories front and center.

  • Wind, gusts and seas for the next five days
  • Small Craft Advisories and marine warnings highlighted
  • Wind and seas chart, plus the full forecast in plain English
Moon

Flip it over. Watch the moon move the sea.

One tap flips the app to a night sky with tonight's moon, lit for its exact phase and animated through the lunar cycle. See where it sits in your sky and how it drives the tide.

  • Moonrise, moonset and when it's overhead
  • Spring and neap tides explained, with king-tide alerts
  • Scrub ahead or back to see the phase on any date
Fishing

Fish when they're feeding.

Solunar major and minor periods with a daily rating, matched with what the tide is doing at each one. Add first and last light, and live water temperature, wind and barometric pressure from the nearest NOAA sensors.

  • Daily fishing rating, from slow to excellent
  • Pressure trend, with what it usually means for the bite
  • 14-day chart of tidal range against the moon's phases

Surf alerts

A heads-up before it gets good.

Turn on surf alerts and Lunatide watches every nearby break for you. About 90 minutes before a Good or Epic session, you get a notification with a card of the break, the wave height, the swell and the wind. Open the app while a session is coming up and a "Surf's up" banner slides in.

On your Mac

A screen saver that's actually worth watching.

The Lunatide screen saver brings the living coastline to your Mac: the circling satellite flyover, live weather, animated tide water, a clock, the current height and the next highs and lows. Pick any beach in its options.

Support

Answers to common questions. Still stuck? Email me and I'll help.

Where does Lunatide work?

Tide predictions come from NOAA, which covers U.S. coasts and territories, and weather comes from the National Weather Service. Surf forecasts work on most coastlines.

Why is my tide station a few miles away?

Lunatide uses the closest NOAA prediction station. Tap the location name at the top of the app to search for a specific beach, harbor or address.

How do I turn on surf alerts?

Open the Surf forecast and turn on Surf alerts at the bottom. Choose "Good or better" or "Epic only," and you'll get a notification about 90 minutes before a session that meets your bar.

How do I add the widget?

Press and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and search for Lunatide. The large widget shows the tide curve, moon phase, fishing rating and the next highs and lows.

Which surf breaks are included?

Over 140 named breaks across California, Oregon, Washington, Hawaii, the East and Gulf coasts, Puerto Rico and the Abacos. Lunatide shows up to six within about 37 miles of you, ranked by how good they are right now.

Where does the boating forecast come from?

It's the National Weather Service Coastal Waters Forecast for the marine zone you're in, with active advisories. Calm, Moderate, Choppy and Rough are Lunatide's at-a-glance summary of the forecast wind and seas; always read the full forecast and use your judgment before heading out.

How is the surf rating worked out?

It combines wave size, swell period, whether the swell can reach your beach, wind direction relative to the shore, and the tide. Lunatide works out which way each beach faces automatically.

Can I use it for navigation?

No. Lunatide is for planning and enjoyment. Tides are predictions, and real water levels vary with wind, pressure and runoff. Always use official charts and current conditions on the water.

Need a hand?

Email the place you were checking and a screenshot if you can. You'll usually hear back within a couple of days.
