Tides

Know the water before you get there.

The current height, whether it's rising or falling, and a live countdown to the next high or low, from official NOAA predictions for the station nearest you.

  • 24-hour tide chart with day and night shading
  • Plus and minus heights, change per tide, and minus-tide badges
  • Tide flow: incoming or outgoing, fastest flow and slack water
  • Two weeks saved on your phone, so it works with no signal
Surf

Catch the good sessions. Skip the blown-out ones.

A 7-day surf forecast rated from Flat to Epic, tuned to the direction your beach actually faces, so offshore and onshore wind are judged the way surfers judge them.

  • Wave height, swell period and direction, hour by hour
  • Best sessions highlighted for every day
  • Surf alerts about 90 minutes before a good session
Moon

Flip it over. Watch the moon move the sea.

One tap flips the app to a night sky with tonight's moon, lit for its exact phase and animated through the lunar cycle. See where it sits in your sky and how it drives the tide.

  • Moonrise, moonset and when it's overhead
  • Spring and neap tides explained, with king-tide alerts
  • Scrub ahead or back to see the phase on any date
Fishing

Fish when they're feeding.

Solunar major and minor periods with a daily rating, matched with what the tide is doing at each one. Add first and last light, and live water temperature, wind and barometric pressure from the nearest NOAA sensors.

  • Daily fishing rating, from slow to excellent
  • Pressure trend, with what it usually means for the bite
  • 14-day chart of tidal range against the moon's phases

Support

Answers to common questions. Still stuck? Email me and I'll help.

Where does Lunatide work?

Tide predictions come from NOAA, which covers U.S. coasts and territories, and weather comes from the National Weather Service. Surf forecasts work on most coastlines.

Why is my tide station a few miles away?

Lunatide uses the closest NOAA prediction station. Tap the location name at the top of the app to search for a specific beach, harbor or address.

How do I turn on surf alerts?

Open the Surf forecast and turn on Surf alerts at the bottom. Choose "Good or better" or "Epic only," and you'll get a notification about 90 minutes before a session that meets your bar.

How do I add the widget?

Press and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen, tap Edit → Add Widget, and search for Lunatide. The large widget shows the tide curve, moon phase, fishing rating and the next highs and lows.

How is the surf rating worked out?

It combines wave size, swell period, whether the swell can reach your beach, wind direction relative to the shore, and the tide. Lunatide works out which way each beach faces automatically.

Can I use it for navigation?

No. Lunatide is for planning and enjoyment. Tides are predictions, and real water levels vary with wind, pressure and runoff. Always use official charts and current conditions on the water.

Need a hand?

Email the place you were checking and a screenshot if you can. You'll usually hear back within a couple of days.
